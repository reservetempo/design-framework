    Aqua

    Aqua is the look of Mac OS X, made by Apple and used from 2001 to 2013: glossy “lickable” buttons, pinstriped windows, and red, yellow and green gumdrops in every title bar.

    This is Aqua as a small CSS library, aqua.css, built from the Aqua look of the admin framework, with its 21 colours. Pick one below or from the Colour menu, and press Tab to see the focus rings. Not affiliated with Apple.

    How to use

    Copy aqua.css next to your page and link it:

    <link rel="stylesheet" href="aqua.css">

    A window is a title bar with the three gumdrops and a title, then a body:

    <section class="aqua-window" aria-labelledby="t1">
  <div class="aqua-titlebar">
    <span class="aqua-lights" aria-hidden="true">
      <span class="aqua-light"></span>
      <span class="aqua-light aqua-light--min"></span>
      <span class="aqua-light aqua-light--zoom"></span>
    </span>
    <h2 class="aqua-title" id="t1">Title</h2>
  </div>
  <div class="aqua-window__body">
    <p>Hello.</p>
    <button class="aqua-button aqua-button--default">OK</button>
  </div>
</section>

    For a colour, put data-color="1" to "20" on <html> (none, or "0", is Classic), or set the five tokens --aqua-face, --aqua-muted, --aqua-title, --aqua-title-text and --aqua-ink yourself. Add class="aqua-desktop" to <body> for the page in the colour. The comment at the top of aqua.css gives the markup for every component.
